# Batch 7 · Legs, back (glutei e femorali)

Movements: extending the hip (bridges, hip thrusts, hinges) and bending the knee against
resistance (leg curls). Main muscles: **gluteus maximus** and the **hamstrings**. Calisthenics
often under-trains this side of the body because squats and lunges are mostly quadriceps.

## What differs between them

- **Hip-led (bridge, hip thrust, single-leg RDL) vs knee-led (sliding leg curl, Nordic curl):** the hamstrings do both jobs, so rotating the two kinds trains them fully.
- **Hip thrust vs squat:** the hip thrust activated the glutes about twice as much and the hamstrings more than the squat, with similar quadriceps (source B). Good for the Legs focus so it's not all quadriceps.
- **Nordic curl:** very hard, but including it in training programmes roughly halved hamstring injuries across 8,459 athletes (source E). Start with a few slow reps.
- **Iso option:** the glute bridge held at the top is the isometric partner for the dynamic rows.

## Sources

- **A · study.** Ekstrom et al. (2007), JOSPT: bridge and unilateral bridge among the 9 exercises measured for glute and hamstring activity. [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18560185/)
- **B · study.** Contreras et al. (2015), *A comparison of gluteus maximus, biceps femoris, and vastus lateralis EMG activity in the back squat and barbell hip thrust*, J Appl Biomech 31(6):452–458. Hip thrust: upper glute 69.5% vs 29.4% (mean), lower glute 86.8% vs 45.4%, biceps femoris 40.8% vs 14.9%; vastus lateralis similar. [Human Kinetics](https://journals.humankinetics.com/view/journals/jab/31/6/article-p452.xml)
- **C · study.** *Effects of loading positions on the activation of trunk and hip muscles during flywheel and dumbbell single-leg Romanian deadlift exercises* (2023): gluteus maximus, gluteus medius, biceps femoris, erector spinae and adductors measured. [PMC](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC10716453/)
- **D · coaching.** Sliding leg curl: hips up in a bridge, slide the heels out and back; hamstrings and glutes; keep the hips high. [Bodybuilding Wizard](https://bodybuilding-wizard.com/sliding-leg-curl-hamstring-exercise/)
- **E · study.** van Dyk, Behan & Whiteley (2019), *Including the Nordic hamstring exercise in injury prevention programmes halves the rate of hamstring injuries: a systematic review and meta-analysis of 8459 athletes*, Br J Sports Med 53(21):1362–1370. [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/331367089_Including_the_Nordic_hamstring_exercise_in_injury_prevention_programmes_halves_the_rate_of_hamstring_injuries_A_systematic_review_and_meta-analysis_of_8459_athletes)
- **F · reference + coaching.** Lateral lunge: gluteus maximus, quadriceps and adductors main movers; gluteus medius when stepping out. [ISSA](https://www.issaonline.com/blogs/training-tips/how-to-do-side-lunges-work-your-quads-hamstrings-glutes), [Peloton](https://www.onepeloton.com/blog/lateral-lunge)

**Limits:** source C used weights (dumbbell and flywheel), not bodyweight, so it shows which
muscles work, not how hard they work at bodyweight. The sliding leg curl relies on a coaching source.

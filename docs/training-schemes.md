# Training schemes: modules and skeleton

**Status: to review.** An analysis of the 12 trainings (Allenamento 1–12), as a base for building
the Suggest and Create sections on the same structure.

## The idea

- **The basket:** the approved exercises (`docs/exercises/`). It grows over time, from 7 per
  muscle group to more and more.
- **The modules:** the types of block a training is built from (Tabata, EMOM, …), each with its
  own timings and rules for which exercises go in it.
- **The skeleton:** the order of the modules in a training.
- **Suggest** fills the skeleton by itself. **Create** lets you choose the modules for each part of
  the skeleton. Both pick exercises from the same basket and follow the same rules.

## The skeleton

```
Warm-up  →  OPENER  →  SKILL SECTION  →  FINISHER  →  Stretching
            1 block    1–3 blocks        1 block (2 in long sessions)
```

| Part | Module options | How often in your trainings | Length |
|---|---|---|---|
| Opener | M1 strength intervals (most common) · M2 EMOM · M7 circuit | M1 8×, M2 2×, M7 1× | 4–8 min |
| Skill section | M4 skill holds (almost always handstand) · M3 skill intervals before it | M4 in 9 of 12; M3 in 6 of 12 | 4–15 min |
| Finisher | M6 AMRAP · M5 Tabata · M7 circuit | AMRAP 6×, Tabata 4×, circuit 2× | 10–12 min |

### Rules seen in the opener and skill section

- **The opener is always pull + push**, alternated, sometimes with a pull hold (your rules 1 and 2).
- **The skill section is shoulders + core:** handstand work, pike / HSPU, planche lean, hollow, L-sit.
- **Legs come almost only in the finisher** (jump squats, pistols, squats), which fits "legs aren't the core of calisthenics".
- **The finisher mixes all groups**, and is the only part with 5–8 exercises.

### Timing

Your trainings add up to about **25–40 minutes of work**, without warm-up and stretching. For the
app's durations, that suggests:

| Session | Warm-up | Opener | Skill section | Finisher | Stretching |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 45 min | 5' | 1 block (~6') | 1–2 blocks (~10') | 1 block (10–12') | 4' |
| 60 min | 6' | 1 block (~8') | 2 blocks (~15') | 1 block (12') | 5' |
| 90 min | 8' | 1 block (~8') | 3 blocks (~20') | 2 blocks (e.g. Tabata + AMRAP, or AMRAP + short EMOM as in training 9) | 7' |

## What changes in the app

Today Suggest and Create don't follow this skeleton:

- **Suggest** uses a fixed list of blocks that has *two* finishers even at 45 and 60 minutes (Tabata + AMRAP), and its intervals don't follow the opener and skill rules.
- **Create** lets you pick any mix of block types and puts them in a fixed order. The result doesn't look like your trainings.

The proposal:

1. **The 7 modules become the building blocks of the app**, each with its default timings (from the table above) and its own rules for choosing exercises from the basket (opener = pull + push; skill = shoulders + core; finisher = everything).
2. **Suggest** fills the skeleton by itself for 45 / 60 / 90 minutes, varying the opener and finisher between sessions using your history (e.g. not AMRAP three times in a row).
3. **Create** shows the same three parts: you pick the opener (strength intervals, EMOM or circuit), how many skill blocks and of which kind, and the finisher (Tabata, AMRAP or circuit), plus duration and focus. Timings stay editable.
4. **Your 12 trainings** stay as they are, each block tagged with its module, so they can serve as a check that the generated ones look like yours.
